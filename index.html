Beyond Traditional Extermination

Milton Rodent Shield was established to modernize structural pest defense. Traditional pest control relies heavily on indoor poison baits that leave decaying rodents inside wall cavities and fail to stop new mice from entering.

Our team combines structural building knowledge with infrared imaging and non-corrosive steel hardware cloth to physically seal every potential entry point down to 1/4 inch, delivering guaranteed, long-term exclusion.
